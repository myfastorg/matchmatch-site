Child Safety Standards

Match! Match! is an adult dating app operated by Rapid DevOps LLC. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we hold ourselves to, how we prevent, detect and report such content, and how to reach us.

1. The app is for adults only

  • Match! Match! is rated 18+ and is only intended for users aged 18 and over.
  • Date of birth is required at sign-up; anyone under 18 cannot complete registration.
  • Every profile — including every photo — is reviewed by a human moderator before it becomes visible to other users. Profiles that appear to belong to a minor are rejected and the account is removed.
  • If we learn that an existing account belongs to a minor, we terminate the account and delete its content.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly prohibited and lead to immediate account termination:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form — images, video, audio, text or links.
  • Sexualisation of minors, including drawings, AI-generated imagery and "age-play" content presented as involving minors.
  • Grooming, sextortion, solicitation of minors, or facilitating access to minors.
  • Trafficking, or offering or seeking sexual services involving minors.

3. Prevention and detection

  • Human moderation before publication. Photos and profile text are checked by our moderation team before a profile enters discovery.
  • User reporting. Every profile, chat and message can be reported from inside the app (menu in the top right → Report), with a dedicated reason for child safety concerns. Users can also block anyone instantly.
  • Per-chat media controls. Photos, voice messages, video notes and calls are blocked in a new chat until the recipient explicitly allows them.
  • Re-review on change. A profile that changes its photos goes back through moderation.
  • Ban enforcement. Accounts terminated for child safety violations are recorded so the same person cannot simply re-register.

4. Response and reporting

  • Reports of child safety concerns are prioritised and handled by our team within 24 hours.
  • Confirmed CSAE content is removed, the account is terminated, and the related data is preserved as required for reporting.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) at report.cybertip.org and cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities.
  • Anyone can report a child safety concern to us directly at fastdevevelopment@gmail.com, whether or not they use the app.

5. Compliance

These standards are aligned with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and with applicable child protection law in the markets where the app is distributed. We review them at least annually and whenever our product or the applicable rules change.
