Delete your Match! Match! account

Match! Match! is a dating app operated by Rapid DevOps LLC. You can delete your account and the data associated with it at any time, directly in the app or by writing to us.

App: Match! Match! (com.senseigai.datingapp)

Developer: Rapid DevOps LLC

Support: t.me/xelp_help · fastdevevelopment@gmail.com

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Match! Match! and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the gear icon in the top right to open Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Delete by request

If you cannot open the app, write to t.me/xelp_help or fastdevevelopment@gmail.com from the e-mail address on your account and ask us to delete it. We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, date of birth, gender, city, height, occupation and other questionnaire answers.
  • Your photos and any media you uploaded.
  • Your messages, voice messages, matches, likes and stories.
  • Your e-mail address, password hash and push notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and transaction records — kept for up to 5 years in an anonymised form, because tax and accounting law requires it. They are no longer linked to your profile.
  • Abuse and moderation records — if your account was reported or banned, we keep a minimal record (an identifier and the reason) for up to 12 months to prevent the same person from re-registering.
  • Server logs — technical logs containing an IP address are rotated and erased within 90 days.

Nothing else is retained. Backups that still contain your data are overwritten within 30 days.

Purchases

Deleting your account does not refund purchases. Full Access is a one-time, non-renewing purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from — Google Play (Google Play refunds) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).
